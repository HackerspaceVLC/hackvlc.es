# From zero to programming with agentic AI

**More information and date coming soon.** Scan the QR code on the poster to stay up to date.

A beginner workshop to take your first steps in programming with AI agents, starting from scratch.

In this hands-on session we will cover:
- What agentic AI is and how it differs from a chatbot
- Fundamentals to start programming with agents
- Common tools and workflow
- Guided hands-on exercises

**Workshop requirements:**
- Bring your laptop
- No previous AI experience required
- Basic programming knowledge recommended, but not essential

**Location:**
- C/ de Francesc Martinez, 19, 46020 València

**How to sign up:**
- Spots will open once the date is confirmed. Scan the QR code on the poster or check the [Hackerspace Valencia Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/es-ES/hackerspace-valencia/) to sign up.

**Important notes:**
- Limited spots available
- Please cancel your registration if you can't attend
