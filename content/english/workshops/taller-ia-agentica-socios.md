The members' version of the agentic AI workshop. Same base as the [open workshop](/en/workshops/taller-ia-agentica/), but more hands-on, across several sessions and in a small group, so we can go at ease and spend time on your own cases.

If the open workshop left you wanting more, this is the follow-up: we move from the demo to setting up your own workflow, with room to practise, ask questions and work on whatever you bring.

**Dates and capacity:** to be announced. There will be several sessions and spots will be very limited, so we can give everyone proper attention.

## What changes compared to the open workshop

- More hands-on, less demo: we set the workflow up with you, not just watch it.
- Across several sessions, with room for questions and for your own cases.
- Small group.

## Why become a member?

- A key and access to the space whenever you want, at reasonable hours.
- A vote in the association.
- Access to the members' Telegram.
- Take part in group projects.
- Members-only workshops, more hands-on and in small groups (e.g. agentic AI).

## How to sign up

Sign-up will be announced on the [Hackerspace Valencia Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/es-ES/hackerspace-valencia/) once we confirm the dates. If you are not a member yet, you can [become one here](/en/pages/alta/).
